    About

    I am a third-year PhD student in Machine Learning at Imperial College London on the AI for Healthcare CDT programme.

    My research focuses on cross-modal learning, interpretable machine learning, and foundation large models for fibrosing lung diseases. I explore how these approaches can enable more precise disease quantification, characterise disease progression, and improve prognosis.

    Outside of research, I love skiing, football, and sauna. I also love to play games, with backgammon, poker, and mahjong being my favourites.
